Page 06 / Ecosystem Depth

"Beyond Team Formation."

SkillSphere powers three interconnected pillars that keep campus talent engaged before, during, and after competitions.

Core Module Implemented

Skill Exchange

Bi-directional matching engine pairing students who want to learn a skill with peers and seniors who can teach it.

Live Capability: Profiles tag Can Teach (e.g. Python) & Want to Learn (e.g. Next.js). Similarity logic pairs reciprocal learners for zero-cost mentorship.
Event Engine Implemented

Campus Events

Direct bridge connecting event discovery with instant squad creation. Discover an event, check match %, and recruit teammates.

Live Capability: Searchable hackathon & workshop feed with one-click registration state persistence and embedded 'Find My Team' launchpad.
Community Hub MVP Built

Campus Communities

Curated interest circles (AI/ML, Web, Robotics, Sports, Design) giving students a noise-free space for discussions and micro-posts.

Live Capability: 280-character micro-posts with embedded media support, eliminating WhatsApp spam while keeping conversations domain-focused.
