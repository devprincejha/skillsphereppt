def cosine_similarity(v1: np.ndarray, v2: np.ndarray) -> float:
    return float(np.dot(v1, v2) / (np.linalg.norm(v1) * np.linalg.norm(v2)))

10 / Bulletproof Defense

Judges Q&A Strategy

Honest, technically accurate, and concise answers prepared for the hardest judging questions.

Q1 Did you train your own AI model?
Answer: "No, and that was a deliberate architectural choice. We leverage pretrained Gemini embeddings for high-dimensional semantic vectorization and designed our own similarity algorithm, complementary team heuristic, and context grounding pipeline."
Q2 Can the AI hallucinate campus events?
Answer: "Hallucinations are minimized by strict retrieval grounding. Ask Campus AI is provided only with verified DB records (events and approved posts) with strict system prompt boundaries: 'Answer strictly from context, if absent reply do not know'."
Q3 How will you solve the Cold Start problem?
Answer: "We partner directly with existing high-activity student bodies (e.g. Access Denied, Drone Club, Google Developer Groups) on Day 1. Instead of convincing 5,000 individuals, we onboard 4 clubs whose members already form active teams."
Q4 How will this scale beyond 25 demo users?
Answer: "Our MVP uses in-memory NumPy vectors for instant sub-second hackathon responses. At 10k+ scale, we migrate to PostgreSQL pgvector indexing with HNSW cosine search and Redis caching for LLM match explanations."
